Privacy policy, GSS Budget Receipts

Last updated: 2026-11-26

What this is

GSS Budget Receipts is part of the Budget section of a private household budgeting tool. It reads receipt emails from a connected Gmail account, and photos of paper receipts that you upload, and matches them to bank transactions, so each purchase can be given a category. Only people on an allowlist can sign in.

What Google data it accesses

It asks for these from your Google account:

  • Read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope).
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google account id (the openid scope), which identifies the account.

It searches your mail for messages that look like receipts: messages from a list of sender addresses, or with subject words such as "receipt", "order confirmation", "invoice" or "refund". The search skips spam, trash and drafts. It then reads the text of the messages it finds. It never sends, deletes, labels or changes any mail.

What it keeps

It keeps only the fields it pulls out of each receipt:

  • The merchant, the dates, the currency, the total, tax and shipping.
  • The type of payment and the last four digits of the card, if the email shows them.
  • The order number and the line items (description, amount and quantity).
  • The sender's domain and Gmail's own message id for the email.
  • Which bank transaction the receipt matches, and the category suggested for it.

It also keeps your Google refresh token, encrypted, and some details about the connection: the Gmail address you linked, your Google account id, the Budget login the connection belongs to, its status, the scope you granted, when you agreed, and when it last ran. The key that decrypts the token is not stored in the database.

It does not keep the email body, the subject line or any attachment.

How the emails are processed

The text of each matching email is sent to Anthropic's API, which pulls out the fields listed above. We send it for this purpose only. We then keep the fields and discard the text.

Receipt photos

On the Receipts page you can take or choose a photo of a paper receipt. This does not need a Gmail connection.

  • The photo is re-encoded on your device, which strips its location and camera data, and it is stripped again on the server.
  • It is sent to Anthropic's API, which reads the merchant, date, total and items from it. We send it for this purpose only.
  • It is stored in private Cloudflare storage. It is never public, and only the two people on the Budget can view it, after signing in.
  • It is deleted automatically after 90 days, or sooner when you delete the receipt in the app.
  • The fields read from a deleted receipt can stay in the database's 30-day point-in-time backup.

Who can see it

The people signed in to this household's budget can see the receipt fields on a transaction, including receipts from your mailbox. Only you get the link that opens the original email in Gmail. We do not sell your data, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The data is stored and processed on Cloudflare: a D1 database and Workers.

Disconnecting and deleting your data

In the app, open Budget, then Receipts, and choose Disconnect. You can also tick the option to delete your extracted receipts. When you disconnect:

  • The app stops reading your mailbox.
  • It asks Google to revoke its access, and deletes the stored refresh token.
  • If you chose to delete your receipts, the extracted receipts, their items and matches are removed. If you did not, they stay. Categories already applied to transactions stay either way.
  • The connection record stays, marked as revoked. It holds no token and no Gmail address, but it still holds your Google account id, the Budget login, the scope you granted, when you agreed, and when it last ran.

The app has no way to delete that record yet. To have your stored data deleted, including that record, contact the site owner through goodstuffsoftware.com.

You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GSS Budget Receipts' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
